# ⚡ SwizzSMP Commands

**Abilities**
`/ability1` — use ability 1
`/ability2` — use ability 2
`/ability3` `/ability4` — your second element's abilities (Swizz Sword only)
**Shortcuts:** `Sneak + F` = ability 1 · `Sneak + Q` = ability 2 (hold an item)

**Your swizz**
`/swizz` — see your swizz, energy and cooldowns
`/swizzbook` — recipes + a guide for every swizz
`/loadout` — choose what your energy unlocks
`/withdraw <amount>` — turn energy into items (you always keep 1)
`/swizz hud` — turn the action bar on/off
`/swizz controls` — turn the Sneak shortcuts on/off

**Friends**
`/trust <player>` — your abilities won't hit them (run it again to untrust)
`/trust list` — see who you trust

# 🎒 Items

**Swizz** — right-click to bind it (only if you don't have one yet)
**Energy** — right-click for +1 energy
**Reroller** — right-click to pick a new swizz (you keep your energy)
**Upgrader** — right-click a block twice to start a 20 min ritual. The whole server is told where it is! Be within 5 blocks when it ends or it drops.
**Swizz Mace** — hit a player to turn off their swizz for 60s
**Dragon Egg** — right-click air to scramble everyone's swizz for 5 min

# 🔋 Energy
Start at **2**, max **5**. Kill a player = **+1**, die = **-1**.
1 → Ability 1
2 → Ability 1 recharges faster
3 → Ability 2
4 → Ability 2 recharges faster
5 → Passive
